Prayer times

The times do not match my masjid

Almost always the calculation method. Open Settings and check, in this order:

  • Calculation method — ISNA, Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, Egyptian, Karachi, Moonsighting Committee and others are available. Masjids in the same city often differ here.
  • Asr madhab — Hanafi puts Asr noticeably later than the standard calculation.
  • Per-prayer adjustments — if your masjid is still a few minutes off after the above, nudge any individual prayer by the minute until it matches.

The app says today's times are approximate

At high latitudes the sun may never reach the angle a prayer time is defined by, so there is no exact answer to calculate. Dhuha applies a high-latitude rule and tells you rather than presenting a number as certain. You can change which rule it uses in Settings; this matters most in Canada, the UK and northern Europe in summer.

Friday shows Jumu'ah instead of Dhuhr

That is intended. You can set how many minutes after Dhuhr your masjid begins the khutbah, and Dhuha will flag Friday events that clash with it. Jumu'ah can be switched off in Settings.

Hijri dates and Islamic events

Dates come from the Umm al-Qura calendar and are calculated on your device. Local moonsighting can move an event by a day in either direction, which is what the Hijri offset setting is for — it shifts every Hijri date and event by up to two days, with a live preview of today's date so you can see the effect before you commit.

Calendars

My events are not showing

  • Check calendar access was granted. If you declined, Dhuha still works for prayer times, Hijri dates and Islamic events, and shows a banner offering to enable access.
  • Check Settings → Visible calendars. If you picked a subset during onboarding, anything outside it is hidden.

Editing a repeating event

When you move, edit or delete one occurrence of a repeating event, Dhuha asks how far the change should reach before writing anything. The options differ by platform because the platforms differ: on iPhone you can change this event only, or this and all later events; on Android you can change this event only, or every event in the series. Android's calendar system has no way to change only later occurrences, so the app says what it will actually do rather than offering a choice it cannot honour.

What is the "Fasting" calendar?

If you switch on the voluntary fasting reminders — the white days, or Mondays and Thursdays — Dhuha creates a calendar of its own on your device and fills it with those days. It is never written into a calendar you own, and turning the reminders off deletes it. Prayer times themselves are only drawn inside the app and are never copied into a device calendar.

Notifications

  • Notifications are off by default and are enabled per prayer in Settings.
  • They are scheduled a few days ahead and topped up each time you open the app, which is a limit the phone imposes rather than a choice. If you do not open Dhuha for more than about five days, scheduled alerts will run out until you do.
  • On Android, prayer alerts need permission to fire at an exact time. If they arrive late or not at all, check that exact alarms are allowed for Dhuha in your system settings.

Privacy

There is no account, no analytics and no server. Your calendar and your location stay on your phone, and the app works fully offline. The full policy is at the privacy policy page.

Reporting a problem

Bug reports are welcome and genuinely useful. The most helpful ones say what you expected, what happened instead, and the steps to see it again. Screenshots help. Send them to dhuha.contact@gmail.com.
