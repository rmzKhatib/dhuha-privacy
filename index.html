Privacy Policy for Dhuha

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Dhuha is a prayer-time and calendar app for iPhone and Android. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does with your information. The short version: your calendar and your location stay on your phone, and there is no account, no analytics and no server holding anything about you.

What Dhuha stores, and where

Everything the app keeps is stored on your device only:

  • Your settings — location label and coordinates, calculation method, Asr madhab, high-latitude rule, per-prayer minute adjustments, Hijri offset, notification preferences and which calendars you have chosen to show. These live in a local database on the device.
  • Study-block records — a local note of which events you created as study blocks, so the app can show them differently. It stores the event's identifier, not its contents.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is no backup to a server, because there is no server.

Your calendars

With your permission, Dhuha reads the calendars already on your device (for example iCloud, Google, Outlook or a university Exchange account) so it can show your events against prayer times, and writes back the events you create or edit in the app.

One optional setting has Dhuha create a calendar of its own on your device: a "Fasting" calendar holding the voluntary fasting days, if you switch those reminders on. It is written only on this device, contains only days Dhuha calculated, and is deleted when you turn the reminders off.

This happens entirely through your device's own calendar store. Your events are never copied, uploaded or transmitted anywhere by Dhuha. If you decline calendar access, the app still works — prayer times, Hijri dates and Islamic events all function normally.

Your location

If you allow it, Dhuha reads your location once to work out accurate prayer times for where you are, and stores the resulting coordinates and a city label on your device. It is not tracked in the background, not shared, and not sent anywhere. You can skip this entirely and type a city name instead.

Prayer times are calculated on your device from those coordinates. No request is made to any prayer-time service.

Notifications

Prayer notifications are local notifications, scheduled by the app on your device. Nothing is pushed from a server, and no push token is created or registered.

Network requests

Dhuha makes no network requests to read your data, and none at all in normal use, with one exception:

  • App updates. On launch, the app asks Expo's update service (u.expo.dev), operated by Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), whether a newer version of the app's code is available. This request contains only technical information needed to answer it — the app's project and version identifiers, your platform (iOS or Android) and, inherently to any internet request, your IP address. It contains no calendar data, no location, no identifiers for you, and nothing about how you use the app. If the device is offline the check fails silently and the app carries on working.

Expo's own privacy policy is at https://expo.dev/privacy.

Other than that, the app functions fully offline.

What Dhuha does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in or registration
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone, because none is collected
  • No tracking across apps or websites

Children

Dhuha does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect personal information at all.
